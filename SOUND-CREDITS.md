# Bundled sound recordings

The four emoji tiles play recordings of their pictured subjects. Audio is embedded locally in `demo-sounds.js`; playback needs no external services. Each recording was shortened, trimmed of surrounding silence, given short fades, converted to mono 32 kHz MP3 (64 kbps for Puppy and Car, 96 kbps for Kitty and Duck), and embedded as base64. The app applies the same gain cap and limiter used for family recordings.
