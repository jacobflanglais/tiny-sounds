# Bundled sound credits

All 25 default sounds are bundled locally in `demo-sounds.js` and work offline. Each adaptation is four seconds long, converted to mono 32 kHz MP3 at 96 kbps, normalized to a moderate level, and given short splice fades. Short calls or musical notes repeat with brief pauses to fill the same window; pitch is unchanged. Playback applies a half-second fade-out to defaults and custom recordings, limited to the final half of shorter custom clips. The gain cap and limiter still apply.

Replacements use dedicated sound effects rather than the previous ambient or historical vehicle excerpts. Bird uses a domestic canary song; Monkey uses stump-tailed macaque calls.
